The Prophecy was given by  an ancient Seer, who also created the magical Heroes' Weapons. 

	Dark times draw near. Only the strongest can save the land from what will come. 
	
	One, to fight close, and give the final blow.
	One, to stay back, and exploit the weaknesses that show.
	and One, to help the others, give strength, and tell them what to know.
	
	These three Heroes will unite eachother, and this land, until finally, this battle is won.
